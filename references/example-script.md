# Example script — "You're making AI videos the hard way" (dialogue, ~75 s)

Two original characters over a satisfying looping background: a nervous crab with an old laptop (the skeptic) and a chill builder in a shark hoodie (the expert). Top third: screenshots and motion panels on the exact word. Middle: one-word captions. Bottom: whoever is talking.

| # | Who | Line | Top-third panel (trigger word) | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crab | Bro. I spent two weeks trying to make one AI video. | 14-day calendar filling red, "FINISHED VIDEOS: 0" ("two") | |
| 2 | Shark | Let me guess. ComfyUI? | Browser screenshot of a node graph | |
| 3 | Crab | Nodes everywhere! My laptop sounds like a jet engine! | GPU card, bars pumping, "GPU 87°C" ("jet") | camera shake |
| 4 | Shark | Mine too. Eight gigs of VRAM. And it made nice pictures. | Fan of 4 generated images ("nice") | |
| 5 | Shark | But a picture is maybe ten percent of a video. | "IMAGE = 10%" ("ten") | |
| 6 | Crab | Okay. Then what's the other ninety? | — | side by side |
| 7 | Shark | Voice. Timing. Typography. Sound. And the edit. | Chips popping on each word | side by side |
| 8 | Crab | So I need, like, five more apps? | 5 app tiles with "$$/mo" | side by side |
| 9 | Shark | Nope. One Claude skill. | Claude tile, "1 SKILL" | glitch on "skill" |
| 10 | Crab | A skill? Like, a text file? | — | side by side |
| 11 | Shark | Basically. A recipe Claude follows every single time. | The skill's file tree ("recipe") | |
| 12 | Shark | Step one. The voice comes first. | Waveform with playhead, "VOICE FIRST" | |
| 13 | Shark | Whisper listens, and writes down the exact second every word lands. | words.json scrolling with this video's real timings | |
| 14 | Crab | Wait, wait. So the text hits right on the word? | "BY FEEL ±0.25 s" vs "BY THE CLOCK 0.00 s" | side by side |
| 15 | Shark | To the frame. Nothing placed by feel. | — | side by side |
| 16 | Shark | Then HyperFrames turns it into video. Plain HTML, rendered. | Code scrolling, "HTML → MP4" ("html") | |
| 17 | Crab | But does it, uh, actually look good? | — | side by side |
| 18 | Shark | Three layers. Giant type in the back, the real thing in the middle, labels on top. | 3 planes separating in 3D, each label lighting up on its word | side by side |
| 19 | Crab | And how long did that take you? | — | side by side |
| 20 | Shark | One evening. | Phone playing the finished reel, "1 EVENING" | glitch on "evening" |
| 21 | Crab | Oh geez. I'm going to cry. | — | camera shake |
| 22 | Shark | Two weeks of tools, or one good process. Your call. | "PROCESS > TOOLS" | glitch on "call" |
| 23 | Shark | Don't cry. Comment SKILL, and I'll send it to you. | "COMMENT [SKILL]" button pulsing | pixel dissolve goodbye |

Why it works: a line every 1–4 s, a joke every 10–15 s, a list structure (voice → timing → HTML → layers) so viewers know where they are, every claim shown on screen at the word that says it, and a comment CTA at the end.
